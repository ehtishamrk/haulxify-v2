Paperwork Steals Driving Hours

Common questions
Do you work with owner-operators or only fleets?
We work with both. Single truck owner-operators are actually our most common client — they have zero back office capacity and the most to gain. We scale with you from one truck to fifty.
What TMS and load boards do you support?
All major platforms — DAT, Truckstop, ITS Dispatch, TruckLogics, McLeod, and others. If you have a preferred system we adapt to it. If you have none, we'll recommend and set up the right one.
How does IFTA filing work with Haulxify?
We track your state-by-state mileage each quarter via your ELD data, calculate your fuel tax liability, prepare the report, and file on your behalf — every deadline, without fail. We also flag discrepancies before they become audit triggers.
Can you help if I don't have a factoring company yet?
Yes. We have relationships with several reputable factoring companies and make introductions based on your freight type, volume, and credit. We then coordinate all submissions on your behalf as part of our billing service.
Is there a minimum fleet size?
No minimum. We start with single truck operators all the way up to fleets of 50+. Pricing scales by vehicle count so you always pay proportionally to your size.
Can Haulxify protect against broker fraud?
Yes. We review every rate confirmation before you load, verify broker MC numbers and bond status, and flag terms that signal double-brokering or non-payment risk. We've caught fraudulent brokers for multiple clients before a single mile was turned.
